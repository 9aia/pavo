pavo is the org's surface — the visualization host every engine drivers into. Products ship tamias-driver-* executables emitting a declarative ViewNode IR; pavo interprets the same tree on CLI, TUI, and web. It owns visualization hosting and nothing else: product state stays with the products, distribution with tamias, policy with peregrin.
